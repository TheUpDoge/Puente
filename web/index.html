          INSIDE PUENTE

          The book is on the phone. A line is cut into the biggest pieces it knows, then said in one language.

          1. 1. Ear The phone listens until you pause. Growing guesses fold into one sentence instead of stacking up.
          2. 2. Turn Me locks English, Them locks Spanish. Auto guesses, and Auto is the one that misses.
          3. 3. Book Tejano phrases, shop talk and slang match the longest chunk they can. Spanglish is fine: words already in the other language are kept as said.
          4. 4. Mouth A full line is spoken once and left big on the screen. A half line stays quiet.
